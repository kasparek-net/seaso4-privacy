Seaso4

Know how far along the season is.

Seaso4 answers a question you never quite know the answer to: how much of this season is left. A half-circle gauge shows the progress in percent or in days, next to NOAA-accurate sunrise and sunset times for wherever you are. The widgets sit on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and in StandBy, and roll over on their own at midnight — there is nothing to configure.

Both definitions of a season are in there, side by side: astronomical, which turns on the solstices and equinoxes, and calendar, which turns on the first of the month. Your location can follow you or stay pinned to Tokyo, Oslo or Buenos Aires. Built for iOS 26, light and dark.
